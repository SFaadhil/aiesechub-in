# Brag Plan: AIESEC India Hub — "One Hub" (Portrait / Apple-keynote cut)

## What is this app?
The central knowledge & resource hub for AIESEC in India (term 26.27) — national search, every portfolio's resources, and a live RnR leaderboard for 19 LCs and 2,000+ members, in one place.

## The angle
An Apple-keynote-style launch cut: black stage, giant type that SLAMS in one line at a time, hard cuts, quick zoom-punches, and a driving beat. Fast, loud, sharp — but every reveal is a real piece of the product (numbers, the search bar, portfolio colors, the leaderboard), so it reads as a genuine launch, not a hype-reel with no substance. The AIESEC blue becomes the "product glow" against black.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Pure black. One word punches to full screen with a blue glow and a hard impact — **"EVERYTHING."** — then a fast cut to **"IN ONE PLACE."** Immediate, loud, keynote-grade.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Big-number slams** — "19 LCs", "2000+ MEMBERS", "1 HUB" fill the screen one at a time, each on a hard beat.
- **Search, fast** — the pill search bar on black; "iGV matching guide" types in a blink and suggestions flash in.
- **Portfolio colors fly in** — iGV, oGV, iGTae, MKT, BD, PM, FnL chips snap into a stack in their real brand colors — "EVERY PORTFOLIO."
- **Leaderboard snap** — real LC rows (Manipal, Delhi-IIT, Pune) hard-cut in with tier badges — "RANKED NATIONALLY."
- **Three-word rhythm** — "SEARCH. / RESOURCES. / RECOGNITION." rapid-fire.

## Outro / punchline
Black. The AIESEC walking-figure mark glows up, "AIESEC India Hub" locks in with "Term 26.27", tagline "Everything your LC needs.", and the URL **aiesechub.in** lands on a final impact. Music drops out on the last hit.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (the hub exists) → key action (search a query, browse portfolios) → result (find resources, see national standing). Centerpieces are the real search bar, the portfolio color set, and the leaderboard rows — recreated from `app/page.js`, `lib/data.js`, and the RnR dashboard.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic (pacing/structure) fused with cinematic production values
- Creative direction: **Apple product-launch keynote — fast, loud, sharp; black stage, giant type, blue product-glow**
- Interpretation: 8 rapid scenes, several under 2s, hard cuts + white-flash cuts + zoom-punch entrances. Type is huge, bold, centered, one idea per cut. Loud but still legible — key words hold ~0.8s. Restraint lives in the palette (black + one blue glow + real portfolio colors), not in the pace.

## Format: vertical / portrait — 1080x1920
## Duration: 19 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: driving, loud rhythmic bed (chaotic energy) with a dense, punchy SFX layer of hard impacts + whooshes, resolving on a final bell
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (most energetic), volume ~0.4, hard stop/quick fade on the final impact
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Tempo ~120 BPM (~0.5s beat grid from 3.02s). Strong cues at **17.02, 17.52, 18.52, 20.02s** — lock the **outro logo lock-in near 18.52s**. Snap the fast scene cuts / word slams onto the beat grid (every beat ~0.5s is fine here because most reveals are 1–2 word slams, not sentences — hold each word ~0.8s even when the cut lands on a beat).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle-to-expressive; let the blue product-glow pulse with bass on the big slams. No waveform/equalizer graphics.
- SFX posture: dense, motion-matched, sharp — hard impacts on word/number slams, whoosh (card-slide) on zoom cuts, keypresses on the search, drops on chip/row snaps, one big bell on the logo. SFX 0.6–0.85 volume.
- Audio-coupled moments: every word/number slam (impact on the frame it lands), search typing (keys), portfolio chips (whoosh + drops), leaderboard rows (snaps), logo lock (bell, beat-locked ~18.52s).
- Restraint rule: loud but not muddy — one clean impact per slam, no stacking three SFX on the same frame; keep it sharp, not noisy. No glitch/error sounds (Apple-clean, not glitchy).
